Maya's Storybook

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

Maya's Storybook (the "App") is made by Grownglow for children ages 3–7 and their families. This policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect.

The short version: the App collects no personal information from you or your child. No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no ad trackers, no analytics.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, locations, photos, voice recordings, or any other personal information from children or parents.

How the App works

  • No account needed. There is no sign-in, registration, or login of any kind.
  • Stories and audio stream from our website (mayastorybooks.com) so the books stay up to date. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • Read-aloud audio is playback only. The App does not access the device microphone and does not record anything.
  • No advertising. The App contains no ads and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics. The App contains no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Children's privacy

The App is designed for children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we collect no personal information from anyone — children included — there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose.

Data security

Since no personal information is collected or stored, there is no personal data at risk in the App.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
