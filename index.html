Meet the Characters

Three friends at the heart of every story.

Maya

The heroine

A six-year-old girl who lives with her Grandmother in a hill village. Kind, generous, brave, and curious — every adventure starts with her.

Moti

The puppy

His name means “Pearl” in Nepali. A scruffy stray Maya rescued on Kukur Tihar, he barks at everything — and his tail goes thump-thump-thump.

Grandmother

The storyteller

The warm heart of Maya’s world. Her bedtime tales become real adventures. She never rushes and never scolds.

Questions families ask

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is Maya’s Storybooks really free?

Yes. Every one of the 20 books is free to read on this website, and the app — when it launches — will be free too: the app, the books, and the games. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

What ages are the stories for?

Children ages 3 to 7. The stories are read-aloud picture books about festivals, kindness, and everyday courage in Nepal, written for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Can I read the stories in Nepali?

Yes. Every book is available in English and in Nepali. On each book page, choose “Read free now” for English or “Read in Nepali” for the Nepali version.

Is there an app?

The Maya’s Storybook app is coming soon for iPhone and Android. Until it launches, every story is free to read right here on the website. Leave your email in the notify box and we will write once, on launch day.

Are there paper books?

Six of the stories are also available as paperbacks on Amazon: Books 1, 2, 3, 4, 10, and 16. Each book page links to its paperback where one exists.
