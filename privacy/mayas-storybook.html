Privacy Policy — Maya's Storybook

Last updated: October 6, 2026
Developer: Grownglow · App: Maya's Storybook (com.grownglow.mayasstorybook)

Maya's Storybook is a read-aloud picture-book app for young children. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with information, in plain language for parents.

The short version
  • The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from children or adults.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no logins.
  • There are no ads and no third-party trackers or analytics.
  • The only thing saved on the device is the child's app preferences (see below), which never leave the phone.

What the app downloads

To show stories, the app downloads picture-book content (covers, story text, narration audio, and music) from our own website:

https://mayastorybooks.com/stories

This is a one-way download of story content. The app does not upload anything to our servers — no names, no device identifiers, no usage statistics.

What the app stores on the device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on the device only, so the app remembers the child's settings between visits:

  • Chosen language (English / Nepali)
  • Screen-rotation and caption preferences
  • Music volume and chosen narrator voice
  • Liked games and dismissed tips

These preferences are stored in the app's private on-device storage. They are never transmitted anywhere and are deleted if the app is uninstalled.

What the app does NOT do

  • No personal information: the app does not ask for or collect names, emails, birthdays, photos, voice recordings, or locations.
  • No advertising: the app contains no ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash SDKs: the app contains no Firebase, Google Analytics, or any third-party data-collection SDK.
  • No permissions beyond internet: on Android the app requests only the standard Internet permission, used solely to download story content. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Maya's Storybook is designed for children under 13 and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Because no personal information is collected at all, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and obtain verifiable parental consent first, as COPPA requires.

In-app purchases (future)

The app is currently free with no in-app purchases. If paid features are added later, purchases are processed entirely by the Google Play Store — we never see or handle payment information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and note the new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means acceptance of the updated policy.
